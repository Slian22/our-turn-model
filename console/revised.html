C1–C9 / REVIEW MAP
What changed, and where to inspect it
Implementation scope follows the corrected contract. These are mechanisms to examine, not validated claims about the world.

    
C1
Adopted efficiency
Capability improves bounded efficiency frontiers. Fixed 1 MW hardware blocks remain physical units; adoption and cohort receipts show deployment.

    
C2
Demand-linked investment
The default planner accounts for surviving capacity, pending deliveries, finite work and resources.

    
C3
Aging and replacement
Age cohorts lose performance and retire. Human replacement, construction, demolition and recycling consume resources.

    
C4
Optional portfolio search
Select the finite-candidate rolling-horizon optimizer instead of C2. Forecasts use observed information and the shared executor.

    
C5
Human support
Population contributes to a saturating service capacity, normalized at the initial population. Capacity and fulfillment differ.

    
C6
Capability difficulty
Rising difficulty slows proportional capability growth. It does not impose a finite capability ceiling.

    
C7
Human technology
A declared, bounded background trend changes critical energy intensity. No historical calibration is claimed.

    
C8
Exposure and mortality
Lagged deficits affect mortality continuously. Population, gross additional deaths and shortage person-years remain separate outputs.

    
C9
Flexible demand
Consumption responds with a lag. Current allocation uses the preceding demand state, preventing circular accounting.
