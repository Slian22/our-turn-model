How to read this comparison

These models run independently. Regional grid settings, industrial work, seeded events and cap or shutdown actions apply only to the regional world. Thomas uses fixed normalized defaults and the selected alignment; a regional policy has no equivalent applied here.

The yellow population curve is Thomas H / initial H multiplied by the regional initial population. This display normalization does not establish matched physical resources, historical calibration or empirical equivalence. The benchmark inspector shows actual normalized stock ratios, including the effects of repair and expansion.
